Lasagna Recipe

Home

Description

A rich and comforting layered pasta dish, lasagna combines layers of tender pasta with a hearty meat or vegetable sauce, creamy cheeses, and a sprinkle of fresh herbs.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • celery stalk, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely sliced
  • 250g pork mince
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 200ml pork stock
  • 200ml red wine
  • 9-12 lasagne sheets
  • 50g Parmesan, grated
  • 150g pack mozzarella, shredded

Steps

  1. In a large pan, heat the olive oil over a low heat.
  2. Fry the onion, carrot, celery and garlic for 5 mins, or until softened.
  3. Add the mince and fry on a medium heat until golden.
  4. Turn up the heat, pour in the wine and bubble until reduced.
  5. Stir in the tomato purée, chopped tomatoes and stock
  6. Preheat the oven to gas 6, 200°C, fan 180°C.
  7. Layer up the lasagne in a baking dish, starting with a third each of the ragu, then the pasta, then the white sauce.
  8. Repeat twice.
  9. Top with the Parmesan and mozzarella then bake in the oven for 40-45 mins, until piping hot and crisp and bubbling on top.
