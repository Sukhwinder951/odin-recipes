Description

These homemade chocolate chip muffins are light, fluffy, and perfectly sweet.

Ingredients

  • 250g self-raising flour
  • 1 tsp bicarbonate of soda
  • 150g chocolate chips milk, white, dark or a mix of all three
  • 100g golden caster sugar

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 200C/180C fan/gas 6 and line a 12-hole muffin tin with paper cases.
  2. Sift the flour and bicarbonate of soda into a large bowl, then stir in the chocolate chips and sugar.
  3. Add the beaten eggs, yogurt and butter, and stir to combine.
  4. Fill the paper cases and bake for 20-25 mins until risen and golden brown.
